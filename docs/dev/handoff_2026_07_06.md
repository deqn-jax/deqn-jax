## Spec-let 1: ELB-region coverage + kink-aware anchor (disaster)

**Why this is the #1 research move:** the house audit's strongest confirmed finding is that the
disaster model *lives* at its effective lower bound — the Taylor rate crosses the floor ~1.18σ
from SS, ~12% of quarters — and the linearization is blind to the kink (slope 0.9999 at SS). So
the BK-anchor's P/Q, the very thing that fixes selection, is the *wrong local model* in the one
region the economy actually visits off-manifold. Every anchor-trained disaster policy is being
pulled toward no-floor dynamics one quarter in eight.

**Design sketch:**
1. *Coverage arm (cheap, do first):* now that coverage∘composite composes (3ed43c7), give
   disaster a `coverage:` block whose stress box targets the ELB region. The stress dims are the
   exogenous drivers that push the Taylor rate down (negative demand/technology states, low
   π/R lags); see spec-let 2 for why you cannot just box the 8 endogenous states.
2. *Kink-aware anchor (the real fix):* replace the single (P, Q) anchor with a two-regime
   anchor: linearize once at SS (floor slack) and once at a floor-binding reference point
   (piecewise-linear OccBin-style local model), gate the anchor loss per-state on which regime
   the state is in (smooth gate on R_taylor − floor). The anchor/jac terms in
   `training/composite_loss.py` are already per-point evaluations over an anchor cloud — the
   change is: two (P, Q) pairs + a gate function, not a new loss architecture.
3. *Measure:* ρ(SS) + SS-consistency (both arms), plus a floor-region held-out grid via
   `ewm_stress_table.py --stress-config` pointed at the ELB box; success = residuals near the
   floor drop ≥1 decade at zero on-measure cost, and IRFs from SS stop drifting
   (memory: disaster IRF-drift finding).
4. *Prereq hygiene:* make `linearize_model` hard-error on BK violation (currently returns
   garbage P with a warning — silent poison for regime-2 linearization experiments); fix the
   in-code "~1e-7 ELB SS distortion" comment (measured −8e-5 log π).

## Spec-let 2: disaster stress-seed map

**Problem:** coverage's stress pool draws box-uniform *states*. irbc's 4 states are all free
coordinates, so a box is a valid state. The disaster model's 13 states include 8 endogenous ones
tied together by equilibrium identities (leverage, net worth, price dispersion, lagged rates) —
a uniform box there generates states the model cannot reach, and the "repair" clip cannot fix
joint infeasibility (it clips marginals). Residuals imposed on impossible states are noise.

**Design:** generate stress seeds by *conditional simulation*, not boxing: (a) draw the 5
exogenous states in a stress box (disaster indicator on, deep negative innovations); (b) roll
from SS through the exact transition under those forced exogenous paths for a short horizon
(the existing `roll_states` machinery, plus a forced-shock variant); (c) the *landings* are the
stress pool — feasible by construction, exactly the paper's "post-shock states" concept. The
piece to build is the forced-exogenous-path roller + a disaster-specific
`stress_shock_ranges` config concept (ranges over shocks/exogenous dims, not states). Half of
`roll_states` already does this.

## Spec-let 3: the identification fix (irbc, then general)

**Problem (referee-diagnosed, [R]):** at δ=0.01 the residual objective has a near-flat direction
— μ's net Euler coefficient 1−β(1−δ)=0.0199, FB flat for μ≫|i|, λ first-order free at SS — so
*every* trained policy (10/10 checkpoints, both arms) is wrong in level at SS while carrying
small residuals. Nothing sampling-side can fix this; the metric can't see the error.

**Candidate fixes, in increasing ambition:**
1. *Reparameterize the outputs:* predict μ̃ = μ·(1−β(1−δ)) (the unit the Euler equation actually
   sees) and/or predict deviations-from-SS with the SS level hard-coded — the flat direction
   becomes a well-scaled direction. Cheap; per-model.
2. *SS pinning loss:* an `aux_ss_level` term |π(s_ss) − y_ss|² (exact, known target; unlike the
   anchor this pins the *level*, not the tangent). One-point anchor, nearly free. Composes with
   coverage (`aux_` prefix auto-excluded from reweighting).
3. *Preconditioned residuals:* scale each equation's residual by the inverse of its policy
   sensitivity at SS (row norms of ∂r/∂y evaluated at SS) — turns the flat direction into a
   round one for ALL models at once; needs care where sensitivities legitimately vanish
   (binding-constraint switch). This is the principled version; do 1–2 first as existence
   proofs.

**Measure:** μ(SS), λ(SS), i(SS) in level vs truth (0, λ*, δ) per seed; the §5 sweep pattern.
The identification diagnosis predicts fix 2 alone repairs levels but maybe not dynamics — that
contrast is itself informative for the doc's §4.3.

## Spec-let 4: restore Alex's disaster (the honest mechanism)

**Why:** the shipped "disaster" is a costume — p = 0 (mechanism inert), θ = 0.05 (≈4.9%
destruction vs Alex's original Barro-consistent 15%), and the original design's σ_d channel
(idiosyncratic dispersion doubling in a crisis: σ_ω 0.268 → 0.54, per the original
`disaster.tex` calibration table, "Risk entrepreneurs credit crisis") was **never
implemented**. Alex's disaster was a *financial* crisis — dispersion doubles, defaults spike,
the BGG accelerator fires — not just a capital haircut.

**Design:** (1) flip defaults or add a config: p_disaster = 0.01, theta_disaster = 0.15
(Alex's numbers; both already plumbed). (2) New constant `sigma_omega_disaster` (default =
sigma_omega ⇒ no-op); in the disaster branch of the mixture expectation, the financial-block
functions (F, G, Γ and derivatives + the ω̄ Newton solve) evaluate at the disaster-state
dispersion. The mixture machinery already computes both branches; the change is threading a
branch-conditional σ through `definitions()` (~30 lines + tests: p=0 bit-identity, σ_d=σ_ω
bit-identity, mixture-weight correctness). (3) Re-solve the risky SS (machinery exists:
`use_risky_steady_state`). **Sequencing:** after spec-let 1 — an active 15% disaster slams the
economy into the ELB region, so the kink-aware anchor should exist first.

**Prior art on the kink (for spec-let 1):** the v0.3.0 probes attacked the ZLB kink from the
network side — `network.use_zlb_feature` (regime coordinate R_lag − R_lb, raw or kinked) and
ReLU activations (`configs/disaster_p10_zlb_zlbfeat.yaml`, `disaster_p10_zlb_relu.yaml`);
their verdicts are not in the tracked record — check old run logs before re-running.
